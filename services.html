Our Services

Transportation for whatever your appointment calls for

From routine checkups to specialized care, we provide safe, dependable rides across Kershaw County.

Core services

Ambulatory Transport

Comfortable, door-to-door rides for patients who can walk or transfer with minimal assistance. Our drivers help you from your door to the vehicle and into your appointment.

  • Door-to-door pickup and drop-off
  • Assistance in and out of the vehicle
  • Round-trip or one-way booking

Wheelchair-Accessible Transport

Specially equipped vehicles and drivers trained to safely secure and transport wheelchair users, with safety as the top priority every step of the way.

  • Wheelchair-accessible vehicles
  • Trained in safe wheelchair securement
  • Patient, respectful assistance

Additional services

One Carolina Transit may also support recurring treatments (such as dialysis), longer-distance appointments outside Kershaw County, and non-medical trips.
