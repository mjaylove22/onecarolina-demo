About One Carolina Transit

A local team, committed to getting you there safely

Since 2016, we've provided dependable transportation to Medicaid and Medicare patients across Kershaw County — because getting to your appointment shouldn't be a barrier to your care.

Our mission

We believe reliable transportation is part of good healthcare, not separate from it. Every ride we provide is built around one principle: never compromise a patient's safety to save time or money.

Whether it's a routine checkup or a recurring treatment, our goal is the same — get you there safely, on time, and treated with respect along the way.

Locally owned and operated

Corey Sweetenburg, Owner & CEO

One Carolina Transit is a locally owned and operated business, founded to fill a real gap in reliable transportation for Kershaw County residents. Leadership is hands-on and directly involved in day-to-day operations.

How we vet our drivers

Background screened

Every driver goes through a background screening before joining our team.

Trained for patient care

Drivers are trained specifically to assist ambulatory and wheelchair patients safely.

Held to a punctuality standard

We know a missed appointment isn't just an inconvenience — it can affect your care. Punctuality matters to us.

Rooted in Kershaw County

We've worked alongside local organizations to help expand access to reliable transportation across Kershaw County — because a ride to your appointment shouldn't depend on your zip code.
