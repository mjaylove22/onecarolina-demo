About One Carolina Transit

A local team, committed to getting you there safely

Since 2016, we've provided dependable, private-pay transportation across South Carolina — because getting to your appointment shouldn't be a barrier to your care.

Our mission

We believe reliable transportation is part of good healthcare, not separate from it. Every ride we provide is built around one principle: never compromise a patient's safety to save time or money.

Whether it's a routine checkup or a recurring treatment, our goal is the same — get you there safely, on time, and treated with respect along the way.

Locally owned and operated

Corey Sweetenburg, Owner, Chief Executive Officer

One Carolina Transit is a locally owned and operated business, founded to fill a real gap in reliable transportation for the communities we serve. Leadership is hands-on and directly involved in day-to-day operations.

How we vet our drivers

Background screened

Every driver passes a national/federal criminal background check, a sex offender registry check, a 10-panel drug screen, and a 10-year driving record review.

Trained for patient care

Drivers are CPR-certified and trained in defensive driving and wheelchair assistance.

Held to a punctuality standard

We know a missed appointment isn't just an inconvenience — it can affect your care. Punctuality matters to us.

Licensed and regulated

One Carolina Transit is licensed with the South Carolina Office of Regulatory Staff (ORS) and holds Public Service Commission Certification #9156-A. We're licensed to operate across three states as needed.
